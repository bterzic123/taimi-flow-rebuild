# Growth Audit — Taimi

**Live:** https://bterzic123.github.io/taimi-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas to unlock more revenue from the traffic Taimi already has, plus a clickable prototype of the sign-up flow and Plans screen, rebuilt in Taimi's own design. Prepared by Adapty, October 2026.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Show your plans when the profile is done. Today setup ends on an empty People tab and Plans only opens from Profile. | CR +15–20% |
| 02 | Read their answers back instead of showing an empty feed. Gender, sexuality, who they want to date and their age range, shown at work. | CR +10–15% |
| 03 | Offer Taimi Bronze ($0.99 on the App Store) once, in your gift sheet, when someone closes Plans. | ARPU +10–15% |
| 04 | Ask for notifications after the plans, inside the app, not via iOS Settings halfway through setup. | CR +10–15% |
| 05 | Put your 4.4 stars from 215K ratings, 40M+ users and a five-star review on the Plans screen. | CR +10–15% |

Only the sequence changes. Every screen keeps Taimi's own art direction.

## Grounding

The prices, ratings, quotes and feature lines all come from Taimi's own published material: the US App Store listing (rating, rating count, chart position, In-App Purchase prices), taimi.com (user count, countries, portrait photography), the App Store screenshots (the review we quote) and the live iOS screens (Plans features per tier, the gift sheet, the location sheet's Bronze upsell). Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps. They are not measured lift for Taimi.
